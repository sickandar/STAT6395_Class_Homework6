Student exam score dataset.

Each row represents one student. Scores range from 0 to 100.

Columns:
- Gender: male or female
- EthnicGroup: group A, B, C, D, or E
- ParentEduc: parent education level
- LunchType: standard or free/reduced
- TestPrep: completed or none
- ParentMaritalStatus: married, single, widowed, or divorced
- PracticeSport: never, sometimes, or regularly
- IsFirstChild: yes or no
- NrSiblings: number of siblings
- TransportMeans: schoolbus or private
- WklyStudyHours: less than 5 hours, 5 to 10 hours, or more than 10 hours
- MathScore: math test score
- ReadingScore: reading test score
- WritingScore: writing test score
